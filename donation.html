Give Your Clothes a New Purpose.

A piece of clothing you no longer need could become something useful for someone else. Be a part of the ReThread movement by donating your clothes.

Why Donate?

Every year, large amounts of clothing are thrown away. Instead of allowing usable fabric to go to waste, we want to give it another purpose by turning it into useful products such as blankets and pillow covers.

What Can You Donate?

  • Clean and dry clothes.
  • Clothes that you no longer wear.
  • Fabric that can be reused for creative projects.
  • Clothing made from materials suitable for repurposing.

Please avoid donating damp, mouldy or heavily soiled clothes. We want to make sure the materials are safe and suitable for reuse.

How Can You Donate?

Donating is simple! Send us an email expressing your interest in donating clothes. Let us know what kinds of clothes you have and approximately how much you would like to donate.

We will get in touch to discuss the next steps and arrange a suitable way to receive your donation.
