How to Donate ↓
01 / WHY YOUR DONATION MATTERS

Something you no longer need could mean something to someone else.

Donating clothes is a simple way to contribute to ReThread's mission. Suitable fabrics can be reused to create useful everyday products instead of being discarded.

Every contribution helps us work towards reducing textile waste and supporting communities.

02 / WHAT WE ACCEPT

What can you donate?

We welcome clothing and fabric that can be safely reused in our projects.

01

Unused Clothing

Clothes that you no longer wear but are still clean and suitable for reuse.

02

Older Clothing

Older garments with fabric that can be used for creative repurposing projects.

03

Extra Fabric

Clean fabric pieces that may be useful for making blankets, pillow covers and other items.

A small request: Please make sure all donations are clean and completely dry. We cannot accept mouldy, damp or heavily soiled clothing.
03 / THE DONATION PROCESS

Three simple steps.

01

Gather your clothes

Find clean clothing and fabric that you would like to donate.

02

Get in touch

Email us with the type and approximate quantity of clothing you wish to donate.

03

Arrange your donation

We will discuss a suitable drop-off or collection arrangement with you.

AFTER YOUR DONATION

Your clothes don't
stop here.

Once received, suitable clothing will be sorted and prepared. Volunteers will then work on transforming the fabric into useful products that we hope to distribute to people in need.
