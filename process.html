Donate Clothes

THE JOURNEY OF A THREAD

From unwanted clothes
to meaningful creations.

Every donated piece of clothing has the potential to become something useful again. Here's how ReThread turns that possibility into reality.

STEP 01

Collection

The journey begins with people willing to give their unused clothes a second life.

Through our clothing donation initiative, we invite individuals to contribute clothes they no longer need. These donations become the raw materials for our upcycling activities.

What happens during collection?

  • People contact us to express their interest in donating clothes.
  • We arrange suitable ways to receive the donated clothing.
  • Collected clothes are brought together for sorting and inspection.

STEP 02

Sorting and Preparation

Before the transformation begins, donated clothes need to be organised and assessed.

We sort the clothing according to fabric type, condition and suitability for different projects. This helps us decide how each piece can be reused.

Our preparation process includes:

  • Checking the condition of donated clothes.
  • Separating suitable fabrics according to their material and texture.
  • Cleaning and preparing the fabrics before they are used.
  • Setting aside materials that are unsuitable for the planned projects.

STEP 03

Repurposing

This is where creativity comes in!

We bring together a group of volunteers who work collaboratively to transform the collected fabrics into useful household essentials.

Rather than letting the clothes go to waste, we explore different ways to reuse their fabric and give it an entirely new purpose.

What can we create?

01. Blankets

Combining different fabric pieces to create warm, reusable blankets.

02. Pillow Covers

Transforming suitable fabrics into practical and decorative pillow covers.

03. Other Useful Products

Exploring additional creations such as tote bags, cushion covers and cloth bags.

STEP 04

Distribution

The final step is to give our creations a new home.

Once the products are completed, we aim to collaborate with local organisations, shelters and community groups to distribute them to underprivileged individuals and families.

Through this step, the clothes that once went unused can become something valuable in someone else's everyday life.

A CYCLE OF CHANGE

One donation. A new purpose. A meaningful difference.

At ReThread, we believe that giving clothes a second life can help create a more thoughtful, resourceful and compassionate community.
