HOW RETHREAD WORKS

From unwanted clothes
to something wonderful.

Every donated piece of clothing has the potential for a new purpose. Here's how we plan to make that happen.

01 / THE JOURNEY

Four steps. One shared purpose.

Our process brings together donations, careful preparation, creativity and community support.

01

THE FIRST STEP

Collection

The journey begins with people donating clothes they no longer need. We plan to collect these donations and bring them together for sorting.

  • Receive clothing donations.
  • Keep donated items organised.
  • Record the donations received.
02

GETTING READY

Sorting & Preparation

Not every piece of clothing can be used in the same way. We will sort donations according to their condition, fabric and suitability for different projects.

  • Check the condition of clothing.
  • Separate suitable fabrics.
  • Clean and prepare materials.
03

A NEW PURPOSE

Repurposing

This is where creativity comes in! Volunteers will work together to transform suitable fabrics into useful everyday products.

01

Blankets

Useful fabric creations for warmth.

02

Pillow Covers

Repurposed fabric for everyday use.

03

Other Useful Items

More creative ways to reuse fabric.

04

SHARING THE DIFFERENCE

Distribution

Once the products are ready, we hope to distribute them to people and communities who could benefit from them.

  • Identify suitable community partners.
  • Organise the finished products.
  • Coordinate responsible distribution.

MORE THAN JUST REUSE

A simple process
with a meaningful purpose.

Every stage of ReThread is designed around making better use of existing materials while creating something useful for others.
