BE PART OF THE CHANGE

Give your clothes a second life.

Your unused clothes could become something meaningful. Donate them to ReThread and help us turn unwanted fabric into useful items for people who need them.

How to Donate

WHY DONATE?

One donation. A whole new purpose.

Clothes that are no longer needed don't have to go to waste. Through ReThread, we hope to collect reusable clothing and transform the fabric into practical products such as blankets and pillow covers.

BEFORE YOU DONATE

What can you give?

We want to make sure every donation can be safely reused and turned into something useful.

01

Clean Clothes

Donate clothes that are clean, dry and ready to be reused.

02

Reusable Fabric

Older clothes and fabric pieces can still have plenty of potential.

03

Good Condition

Please avoid damp, mouldy or heavily soiled clothing.

Not sure whether an item can be reused? Email us and ask!

THE DONATION PROCESS

Three simple steps.

01

Gather

Collect clothes and fabric you no longer need.

02

Contact Us

Send us an email to let us know what you would like to donate.

03

Arrange

We'll coordinate the donation and discuss suitable drop-off arrangements.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

Your clothes begin a new journey.

Once collected, the clothes will be sorted and prepared. Suitable fabrics will be repurposed into useful items that we aim to distribute to people in need.
