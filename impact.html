THE DIFFERENCE WE HOPE TO MAKE

Small contributions.
Meaningful change.

At ReThread, we believe that even the smallest contribution can become part of something bigger. By bringing people together, we hope to create positive change for both the environment and our communities.

01. FOR THE ENVIRONMENT

Giving textiles another purpose.

Clothing waste is a growing environmental concern. When reusable textiles are discarded, valuable materials are lost.

Through ReThread, we aim to encourage people to think differently about unwanted clothes. By reusing existing fabrics and turning them into new products, we hope to reduce the amount of clothing that goes to waste.

  • Encouraging clothing reuse rather than disposal.
  • Giving existing fabrics a second purpose.
  • Promoting awareness about textile waste.
  • Inspiring more responsible consumption habits.

02. FOR THE COMMUNITY

Turning creativity into care.

ReThread is not only about reducing waste. It is also about creating useful products that can support people who need them.

By transforming donated clothing into household essentials such as blankets and pillow covers, we hope to provide practical support to underprivileged individuals and families.

We also want to encourage community participation by bringing together volunteers who can contribute their time, creativity and skills.

03. WHAT WE AIM TO ACHIEVE

Our Goals

01

Collect More, Waste Less

Encourage people to donate clothes instead of throwing away reusable textiles.

02

Create Useful Products

Transform donated fabrics into practical everyday household essentials.

03

Bring People Together

Build a group of volunteers who work together towards a common purpose.

04

Support Those in Need

Distribute the finished products through suitable community organisations.

05

Spread Awareness

Help people understand the value of reusing and repurposing textiles.

04. TRACKING OUR PROGRESS

Every contribution counts.

We want our impact to be measurable, not just something we talk about.

As ReThread grows, we plan to keep track of:

  • The total number of clothing donations received.
  • The amount of fabric successfully reused.
  • The number of products created.
  • The number of people who receive donated products.
  • The number of volunteers involved.

These measurements will help us understand our progress, identify areas for improvement and see how our efforts develop over time.

OUR BELIEF

That's what ReThread is all about.
