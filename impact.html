THE DIFFERENCE WE HOPE TO MAKE

Small contributions.
Meaningful change.

We believe that giving clothes another purpose can create positive change for both the environment and people.

01 / WHY OUR WORK MATTERS

Impact isn't always about doing something big.

Sometimes, it begins with something as simple as donating clothes you no longer use. ReThread aims to turn these small contributions into something useful through reuse, creativity and community involvement.

02 / FOR THE ENVIRONMENT

A SECOND LIFE FOR FABRIC

Less textile waste. More thoughtful reuse.

Clothing that is no longer wanted can still contain materials that are useful. By collecting and repurposing suitable fabrics, we hope to reduce the amount of clothing that gets discarded.

01

Encouraging clothing reuse.

02

Making better use of existing fabrics.

03

Promoting awareness about textile waste.

03 / FOR THE COMMUNITY

Turning creativity
into care.

ReThread is not only about reducing waste. It is also about creating useful products that can be shared with people who need additional support.

01 / USEFUL CREATIONS

Everyday essentials

Repurposed fabrics can become products such as blankets and pillow covers.

02 / SHARED EFFORT

Community involvement

Donors and volunteers can contribute their time and creativity to a shared cause.

03 / SUPPORTING OTHERS

Meaningful giving

We hope to distribute finished products to communities and people who could benefit from them.

04 / WHAT WE HOPE TO ACHIEVE
