WHO WE ARE

More than just a clothing donation drive.

Every year, countless clothes are discarded despite having the potential for a second life. ReThread aims to change that.

We bring people together to collect unwanted clothing, creatively repurpose the fabric, and turn it into everyday essentials such as blankets, pillow covers and other useful items.

These creations are then donated to underprivileged communities, giving old textiles a new purpose while making a difference in people's lives.
