THE RETHREAD IDEA
01 / INTRODUCING RETHREAD

Not just old clothes.
A chance to create something new.

ReThread is a clothing reuse initiative that aims to reduce textile waste while supporting people in need.

Through clothing donations, creativity and teamwork, we plan to transform unwanted fabrics into useful everyday items such as blankets and pillow covers.

More about us →
02 / WHY IT MATTERS

One problem.
Two opportunities to make a difference.

01

Textile Waste

Clothes that are no longer wanted can end up being thrown away, even when their fabric could still be useful.

02

Everyday Essentials

Useful items such as blankets and pillow covers can make a difference to people who need additional support.

03

A New Purpose

By reusing donated clothing, we hope to connect these two issues and turn unused fabric into something valuable.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

From your wardrobe
to a new beginning.

Every donation will go through a simple process before becoming something useful.

01

Collect

Gather clothing donations from people who want to give their unused clothes another purpose.

02

Prepare

Sort the donated clothes and prepare suitable fabrics for creative reuse.

03

Repurpose

Work together to transform suitable fabrics into useful products.

04

Donate

Share the finished products with people and communities who need them.
