GET TO KNOW US

What is ReThread?

ReThread is a community-driven initiative that aims to reduce textile waste while supporting underprivileged communities.

We collect clothes that people no longer need and bring together volunteers to transform them into useful everyday essentials, such as blankets, pillow covers and other household items.

Instead of allowing usable fabric to go to waste, we give it a new purpose. The finished products are then donated to people who need them.

We believe that something someone no longer needs could become something someone else truly values.

WHY WE EXIST

Why ReThread?

Every year, enormous amounts of clothing and textiles are discarded, even when much of that material could be reused.

At the same time, many people lack access to basic household essentials.

ReThread hopes to bridge these two issues by connecting people who have clothes to give with people who could benefit from the products those clothes become.

Waste isn't always the end of a story. Sometimes, it's the beginning of another.

THE JOURNEY OF A THREAD

How does it work?

Three simple steps. One meaningful change.

01

Collect

People donate clothes they no longer use, giving unwanted textiles an opportunity for a second life.

02

Repurpose

Volunteers work together to transform donated fabrics into useful products such as blankets and pillow covers.

03

Donate

The finished products are distributed to underprivileged communities, where they can make a difference.

WHAT WE BELIEVE IN

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to give unwanted clothes another purpose while making everyday essentials more accessible to those who need them.

We want to encourage people to rethink the way they view clothing waste and show how small contributions, when brought together, can create meaningful change.
